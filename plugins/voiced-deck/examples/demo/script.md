# Hivewise seed pitch (fictional)

The spoken script for the demo deck. Hivewise is a made-up company and every number is invented.

How to read it:

- `[click]` is a build on the slide. Each slide has exactly as many builds as `[click]` marks.
- `(pause)` is one beat of silence. **Bold** is a word to stress.
- `voiced-deck script script.md deck.html` writes this into the deck.

## 1 · Hivewise. Hear the hive before it fails.

Every spring, beekeepers lift a lid and find a silent hive. (pause) We think that hive was telling them for weeks. Nobody was listening.

[click] Quick note. Hivewise is made up, and so is every number here. The dots still work like a real deck. Solid is measured, half is inferred, dashed is a guess.

## 2 · We lose a third of our hives.

[click] Last winter our pilot beekeepers lost **thirty-eight percent** of their colonies. Varroa mites, a failed queen, a cold snap. [click] And most of those losses were found in spring. **Too late** to do anything.

## 3 · A sick hive sounds different.

Here is the insight. [click] A healthy colony hums. Steady, low, almost boring. [click] A queenless colony **roars**. The pitch climbs and it gets ragged. [click] We hear that change about three weeks before a beekeeper would see it. That number is inferred, from forty hives.

## 4 · A sensor listens. Your phone warns.

So we built the ear. [click] A small sensor sits under the lid and listens all day. [click] A tiny model on the device scores the sound, so it runs for a year on one battery. [click] When something changes, your phone tells you which hive, and what to check.

## 5 · 1,240 hives listen today.

[click] We started in January with forty hives. Today it's **1,240**, all through word of mouth. [click] The dashed line is our guess for December. Six thousand hives. It's a guess, and I'll say so every time.

## 6 · Nine dollars a hive, every month.

[click] The model is simple. **Nine dollars** a hive, a month. [click] A hundred and twelve beekeepers pay us today. We think the margin is about seventy-one percent. Payback in five months is still a guess.

## 7 · We raise $1.2M for 10,000 hives.

So here is the ask. [click] We're raising **$1.2 million**. Almost half goes into sensors, a third into the team, the rest into sales. That buys eighteen months, and ten thousand hives listening. (pause) Thank you. I'd love your questions.
